ROMAN LEICESTER

The wall was part of a public bathhouse.

Jewry Wall formed part of a public bathhouse complex built around AD 160. The surviving wall is original Roman masonry; other stonework on the site is modern and representative.

Source: Jewry Wall Museum · What is here ↗

C. 1390 · VERIFIED SITE HISTORY

A hall with many lives.

The Great Hall was built for Leicester’s Corpus Christi Guild. Over the centuries, the Guildhall also served civic and public roles, including a town library, police station and museum.

Source: Historic England · list entry 1361405 ↗
