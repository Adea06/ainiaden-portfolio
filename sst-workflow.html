Evidence → decision

Each feature answers
a real friction.

The interface was shaped around the moments SSTs were most likely to lose time, duplicate work or miss context.

Interview insight 01

“Who needs contacting today?”

So the landing view prioritises today’s actions, reason for contact and what needs sending.

Workflow insight 02

The same update lived in several places.

So the dashboard is designed as one input point that can sync back to existing Excel records.

Scale insight 03

Large cohorts made manual tracking risky.

So priority, ownership and schedule are surfaced together to reduce searching and missed actions.

The service rhythm

Designed around
the deadline.

The workflow mirrors what SSTs already do, rather than asking them to learn an artificial process.

1 month before

Email + guidance pack

SIMS for cohorts; Outlook for smaller groups. The pack contains the assignment brief, Harvard referencing guide, cover page and module guide.

2 weeks before

Second reminder

Repeat the deadline and relevant support information.

1 week before

Final email

Give students another clear opportunity to submit or request support.

24 hours before

Check Moodle

Identify only students who are not showing as submitted.

Targeted action

Call non-submitters

Focus staff time on the students who still require contact.

Final direction
